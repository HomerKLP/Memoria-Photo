Shapes and sizes

  • A person is a circle. An album, a photo or a month is a square with 10 dp corners (12 on a cover tile).
  • Faces: 72 in a row of people, 68 in a grid, 48 in a sheet head, 40 in a list row. The People screen is the exception: 96 named, 56 unnamed.
  • Rows and sheets keep 16 dp from the screen edge, and 16 dp between a row's icon or picture and its text.
  • A subtitle under a title is 12 sp, in a top bar and in a sheet. Sheets have 28 dp top corners. A search field is 48 dp tall.
  • A picked item shows the accent ring or outline and a check.

Rules that keep the layout still

  • Nothing above a scrollable list may change height between modes. Keep the space, or keep the part visible.
  • A screen shows its last data at once, then reloads. Empty first means the content is short, the scroll clamps, and it never comes back.
  • Hide content below the list, never above it. A bar appearing at the bottom does not move the top.
  • Every screen with modes gets a mockup per mode (list, selecting, empty) so the jump is easy to see.
